Batter basics

Stir until the flour disappears and stop. Lumps are fine. Overmixed batter makes tough pancakes.

Heat and timing

Medium heat browns the outside in two minutes. If the first pancake burns, lower the heat.

When to flip

Flip when bubbles pop on the surface and the edges look dry.
