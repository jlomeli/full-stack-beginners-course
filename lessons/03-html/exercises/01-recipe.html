Buttermilk Pancakes Fluffy pancakes in 30 minutes, made with things you probably have in the kitchen. Photo: a stack of three golden pancakes on a white plate, topped with a pat of butter and syrup. Serves: 4 Prep time: 10 minutes Cook time: 20 minutes Ingredients - 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour - 2 tablespoons sugar - 2 teaspoons baking powder - 1/2 teaspoon baking soda - 1/2 teaspoon salt - 2 cups (480 ml) buttermilk - 2 large eggs - 3 tablespoons melted butter, plus more for the pan Steps 1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl. 2. In a second bowl, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter. 3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ones and stir until just combined. Don't overmix: a few lumps are fine. 4. Heat a pan over medium heat and brush it with butter. 5. Pour 1/4 cup of batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form on top, about 2 minutes, then flip and cook 1 more minute. 6. Serve warm. Tip: No buttermilk? Stir 2 tablespoons of lemon juice into 2 cups of milk and wait 5 minutes. Recipe from the course kitchen. Last updated October 1, 2026.
